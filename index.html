The problem

Two identical ovens. A thousand dollars apart.

Same model · same auction company · same year
Blodgett DFG-100 $3,500 sold for Blodgett DFG-100 $2,500

Forty percent apart. Somebody overpaid by a thousand dollars, or somebody got a thousand-dollar bargain. At the time, neither of them knew which.

It happens on every walkthrough. Two coolers look the same and one is worth three times the other. A Hobart averages nearly four times what a comparable-looking unit brings. The knowledge exists. It just lives in the heads of people who've been doing this for thirty years, and it doesn't scale past one person.

Three numbers, not one: fair market, orderly liquidation, and forced liquidation. What a piece is worth depends on whether you have ninety days or until Friday.
